A Discord bot that lets a server regulate itself. Members nominate each other for roles, and everyone else votes and discusses the outcome in a poll, so roles are earned on merit. Over time it grew to cover what the big bots do: reaction roles, threads for looking-for-group events, and a universal timestamp command.
