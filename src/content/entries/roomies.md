Housemates join a household with a shared ID, then track who does what and who owes whom: rotating chore assignments, recurring bills with a nominated payer and automatic even splitting, shared supplies with a rotating restocker, calendar views of everything due, and per-device due-day reminders that follow the date when a rotation moves. Email and password auth with verification, on Android, iOS and web from one codebase.
